How it works

From the muscles you pick to every rep you lift.

  1. Step 1

    Pick your muscles

    Tap the muscle groups you want to train. Then pick days a week, your experience and your equipment.

    Shown with sample data.

  2. Step 2

    Get a weekly plan

    fitee builds your week: sets per muscle for your level, big lifts first. Rather plan your own? Log your own workouts and routines.

    Shown with sample data.

  3. Step 3

    Prop up your phone

    Side-on for most lifts, front-on for some. A setup check makes sure your whole body is in view, then recording starts and stops by itself. Or use a video you already have.

    Shown with sample data.

  4. Step 4

    See every rep

    fitee counts your reps and checks each one, with a skeleton drawn over your video. It never guesses: when the camera can’t see well enough, it says so. Each fault comes with a cue and an exercise that helps.

    • Passed
    • Fault
    • ?Couldn’t judge

    Sample data: the app’s demo figure stands in for a lifter. Rep 3 stops short of depth. In rep 4 the knee was out of sight, so its depth couldn’t be judged.

  5. Step 5

    The coach adjusts

    Simple rules, on your phone. It adds weight once every set reaches the top of your rep range and your latest check on that lift was clean. The same fault in two of your last three checks? A lighter load, plus an exercise that helps. Lifts stalling? It offers an easier week.

    Sample data: depth was missed in two of the last three squat checks, so the squat is about 10% lighter and a box squat is added.

Your log

Every set, in one place.

Every workout lands in your log with its sets, reps and weights, whether the plan built it or you did.

  • A calendar of every workout
  • Progress charts and records for every exercise
  • A weekly body map, shaded by the sets each muscle got
03

Private by design

No signal down in the basement? fitee doesn’t need one.

No account and nothing to upload: fitee does its work on your phone, so your training stays there too.

  • Works with no signal

    Once it’s on your home screen, the app and its 24 MB pose model are saved on your phone.

  • Video never leaves your phone

    The pose model runs on the phone itself. No uploads, no analytics.

  • Kept only if you save it

    Tap Save video after a check to keep a set, on your phone only. Otherwise it’s gone when you close the results.

  • No account

    Your plans, log and profile stay on your phone. Backups are files you keep.

The vision

Every set logged. Every rep checked. A plan that adapts.

Where fitee is going: a phone app that analyses your lifting and shapes your training around the technique it sees. A log and a coach that adapt the plan. Like a food diary, but for lifting.

It’s early: fitee is a first prototype, in testing now.
