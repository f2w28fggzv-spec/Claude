FAQ

Honest answers

Does REMLET V1 have GPS?

No — and that's on purpose. V1 has no GPS, no SIM card and no location servers: it connects only to your phone over Bluetooth, so there is simply no location data to leak, sell or subpoena. GPS arrives in V2 (2027) as an opt-in family feature with EU-hosted, consent-first infrastructure.

How does a reservation work? Do I pay now?

You pay nothing now. A reservation locks the €25 early-bird price and your place in the first batch. When your bracelet is ready — from mid-October 2026 — we contact you and you pay on delivery. You can cancel any time with one e-mail.

Which app does it use?

At launch, the bracelet pairs with a companion Android/iOS app for alarms and reminders — set-up takes about two minutes and the quick-start card in the box walks you through it. Our own Remlet app for Android follows shortly after launch, with named reminders and distinct vibration patterns.

What about warranty and returns?

EU consumer rules apply in full: a 2-year legal conformity warranty and a 14-day right of withdrawal for distance purchases. The bracelet is CE-marked (RED, RoHS) with skin-safe silicone, and we keep service stock from every batch for quick replacements.

Who is behind REMLET?

Three student founders from Mārupe, Latvia — Martins, Nikolass and Henrijs — running REMLET as a student mini-company (SMU) in the Junior Achievement Latvia program. Every bracelet in the first batch is tested and packed by us personally.
